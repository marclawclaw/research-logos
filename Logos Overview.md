# Logos Overview

## Summary

Logos is a unified decentralised technology stack combining messaging ([[Waku]]), storage ([[Codex]]), and blockchain consensus ([[Nomos]]) layers. Rebranded in November 2025 from three separate projects, it aims to provide privacy-preserving infrastructure for "post-nation-state governance" and censorship-resistant applications.

## Key Facts

> [!fact] Confirmed from official sources
> - **Unification announced:** November 2025
> - **Components:** Messaging (formerly Waku), Storage (formerly Codex), Blockchain (formerly Nomos)
> - **Contributors:** ~200 active, 157 listed on logos.co
> - **Repositories:** 295 across orgs
> - **Circles (local chapters):** 28
> - **Parent org:** IFT (Institute of Free Technology)
> - **Research arm:** VAC (vac.dev → research.logos.co)

### Architecture

- **Modular, plugin-based runtime** — nodes dynamically discover and load modules
- **Privacy-preserving by default** — network-level protection
- **Built on libp2p** — shared networking foundation

### Mission

> [!quote]
> "Logos exists at the intersection of human exploration and technological revolution... giving people tools to explore and innovate using decentralised technology."

The stated goal is "revitalising civil society" through technology that enables:
- Censorship-resistant communication
- Private data storage
- Sovereign governance infrastructure
