# Vac

## Summary

Vac is a principle-driven R&D team that provides comprehensive technical support to Institute of Free Technology (IFT) startups. It develops foundational components, unopinionated protocols, and specifications that [[Logos]] projects and the broader community can utilize.

## Key Facts

> [!fact] Confirmed from official sources
> - Part of the Institute of Free Technology (IFT) ecosystem
> - Maintains specialized R&D service units (VSUs) and incubator projects (VIPs)
> - Custodians of the RFC specifications: https://rfc.vac.dev/
> - Active forum for collaboration: https://forum.vac.dev/
> - Website now redirects to research.logos.co

### Key Outputs

- **Specifications** — RFC-style protocol specs at rfc.vac.dev
- **Research** — Applied research driving protocol development
- **Libraries** — Reusable components for decentralized technologies
- **Publications** — Academic papers and technical reports

### Service Model

Vac offers direct assistance where projects can outsource development of specific protocols or components. This is particularly valuable when components have applications across multiple projects.

## How it relates to Logos

Vac is the **research arm** that supports all [[Logos]] components. It develops the underlying protocols and specifications for [[Waku]], [[Codex]], [[Nomos]], and other projects. The specifications at rfc.vac.dev define how these systems work.
