## Key Facts (Quick Reference)

- **What:** Privacy-preserving, censorship-resistant decentralised storage
- **Architecture:** Decentralised Durability Engine (DDE)
- **Redundancy:** Erasure coding (not replication)
- **Proofs:** ZK proofs (Poseidon2) published on Ethereum
- **Token:** CDX on Ethereum
- **Implementation:** Nim (`logos-storage/logos-storage-nim`)
- **Current status (Mar 2026):** Testnet paused, architecture revision underway
- **Mainnet target:** Originally Q3 2025; delayed
