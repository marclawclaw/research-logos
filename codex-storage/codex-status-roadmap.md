# Codex – Current Status & Roadmap

## Status as of Early 2026

| Phase | Status |
|---|---|
| Non-Incentivised Testnet | ✅ Was live; **paused August 2025** |
| Architecture Redesign | 🔄 In progress (Q3-Q4 2025) |
| Incentivised Testnet | ⏳ Planned (H2 2025 per earlier roadmap) |
| Mainnet | ⏳ Targeted for end of 2025 / 2026 |

> Note: As of August 2025, the team paused testnet to revisit core architecture. New testnet is "on the horizon" — updated timeline not confirmed in available sources.

## Evolution Announcement (July/August 2025)

Codex is undergoing a "significant evolution":
- Transitioning to a new approach **more closely aligned with the wider Logos ecosystem vision**
- Previous testnet gathered community feedback that informed a redesign
- Mission unchanged: censorship-resistant, reliable, developer-friendly storage
- The evolution will impact the broader Logos tech stack positively

This mirrors the rebranding across Logos — Nomos → Logos Blockchain, Codex → Logos Storage Module, Waku → Logos Messaging Module.

## 2025 Roadmap Milestones (from earlier blog posts)

1. **H1 2025**: Non-incentivised testnet (altruistic mode, open to public)
2. **H2 2025**: Incentivised testnet (storage contracts, ZK proofs, collateral)
3. **End 2025**: Mainnet readiness
