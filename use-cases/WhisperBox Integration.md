# WhisperBox — Privacy-Preserving Decentralised Forms

**Website:** https://www.whisperbox.app  
**GitHub:** https://github.com/whisperbox-org/whisperbox  
**Builder:** Waku Team (internal hackathon)  
**Category:** Privacy Tools / Productivity  
**Logos Stack Used:** Waku (+ potential Codex for persistence)  
**Status:** Hackathon MVP — strong product potential flagged

## What It Is

WhisperBox is a decentralised, privacy-preserving form submission platform — a Web3 alternative to Google Forms:

- Create forms, surveys, and polls
- Responses are submitted with **identities protected** (respondent privacy preserved)
- End-to-end encrypted by default
- Built entirely on Waku P2P infrastructure — no centralised backend

## Key Properties

- Lightweight, minimal resource requirements
- Native E2E encryption
- Censorship-resistant
- Permissionless — no central authority manages submissions

## Roadmap Ideas

- Encrypting forms themselves (not just responses)
- Spam protection and incentivisation mechanisms
- **Codex integration** for long-term decentralised storage of forms and responses

## Market Fit

Strong demand exists for this type of tool both within IFT/Logos and in the broader Web3 ecosystem. Traditional form tools (Google Forms, Typeform, SurveyMonkey) offer no privacy guarantees.

## Why This Matters for Logos

- Highly practical, universally relatable use case — easy to explain to non-technical audiences
- Effective showcase of Waku's messaging layer for real-world forms beyond chat
- Shows the "privacy by default" design philosophy of the Logos stack

## Future Integration

Once Codex mainnet is live, the natural evolution is Waku (transport) + Codex (persistence) to make WhisperBox fully decentralised end-to-end — a recurring pattern across Logos apps.
