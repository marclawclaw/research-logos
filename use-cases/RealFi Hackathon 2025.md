# RealFi Hackathon 2025 — Winners & Projects

**Hosted by:** Logos + Funding the Commons  
**Duration:** 23 September – 16 October 2025  
**Theme:** Privacy Technology for Real-World Impact  
**Prize pool:** Up to $4,000 per winning project per track

## Tracks

### 1. Resilient Activist Technology
Building tools to protect activists from censorship and surveillance.

### 2. Logos × Tor Privacy Infrastructure
Advancing Tor-compatible systems that strengthen privacy online.

## Winners

### 🥇 Shielded Micropay (Logos × Tor Track)
**Stack:** Railgun + payment channels  
**What it does:** Private, verifiable billing for services using Railgun for shielded transactions combined with payment channels for micropayments  
**Relevance:** Combines DeFi privacy (Railgun/Waku) with payment infrastructure

### 🥇 Tohaku (Logos × Tor Track)
**Stack:** Tor / Nym + Ethereum wallet  
**What it does:** An Ethereum wallet that routes RPC calls through Tor/Nym to protect users from RPC tracking and metadata leaks  
**Relevance:** Addresses a real privacy gap — most Ethereum wallets leak IP addresses and browsing patterns to RPC providers

## Significance

This hackathon is notable because:
- It shows Waku/Logos being positioned for **activist and civil liberties use cases** — not just DeFi
- The Tor × Logos track demonstrates interest in combining Tor's anonymity network with Logos's P2P comms
- Winners address real attack vectors (RPC surveillance, payment traceability) rather than abstract demos

## Related Context

The RealFi Hackathon was launched as part of the **Logos Privacy in Practice Campaign** with Funding the Commons, focused on the intersection of:
- P2P communications (Waku/Logos Messaging)
- Privacy-preserving payments (Railgun)
- Anonymous networking (Tor, Nym)
