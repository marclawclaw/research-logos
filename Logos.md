# Logos

## Summary

Logos is both a social movement and a unified technology stack built to revitalize civil society through decentralized, privacy-preserving tools. It combines messaging ([[Waku]]), storage ([[Codex]]), and blockchain ([[Nomos]]) into a coherent ecosystem designed for censorship resistance and user sovereignty.

## Key Facts

> [!fact] Confirmed from official sources
> - Logos consists of three main technology pillars: Waku (messaging), Codex (storage), and Nomos (blockchain)
> - As of 2026: 2,237 total contributions, 157 active contributors, 295 repositories, 28 local "Circles"
> - Private-by-default design philosophy
> - Part of the Institute of Free Technology (IFT) ecosystem

- The technology stack is designed to be interoperable — components work together or independently
- Local chapters ("Circles") are at the heart of the movement for civil organizing and skill-sharing
- Annual event: "Parallel Society" — two-day event in Lisbon combining music, art, and technology
