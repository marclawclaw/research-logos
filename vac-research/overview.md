# Vac Research — Overview

> Last updated: 2026-03-14  
> Source: [research.logos.co](https://research.logos.co/)

## What is Vac?

Vac is a **principle-driven R&D team** that provides comprehensive technical support to [IFT](https://free.technology) (Institute of Free Technology) startups, including Logos, Waku, Codex, and Nomos.

Structure:
- **R&D Service Units (VSUs):** Specialized units developing foundational protocols, libraries, and specifications
- **Vac Incubator Projects (VIPs):** Emerging initiatives that spin out from Vac research, eventually becoming standalone IFT projects

## Mission

> "We focus on developing foundational components and unopinionated protocols that both IFT projects and the broader community can utilize."

Key pillars:
1. **Direct assistance** — IFT projects can outsource development of specific protocols or components to Vac units
2. **Applied research** — Bridges theory and practice; not purely academic
3. **Spec stewardship** — All IFT specs managed via RFC/LIP process at [lip.logos.co](https://lip.logos.co/)
4. **Community** — Active forum at [forum.vac.dev](https://forum.vac.dev/)

## Incubator Projects (VIPs)

### Nescience
- Focus: **state separation architecture** enabling privacy-preserving computing
- Particularly important for Logos (the flagship IFT project)
- Arose from ACZ unit's deep ZK research
- Work includes: 23+ zkVM reviews, benchmarking of 6 leading zkVMs, NSSA architecture
- Status: In development within Vac; eventual goal is standalone IFT project
