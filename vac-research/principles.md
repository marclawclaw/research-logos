## Goal

> "The goal of Vac is widespread adoption of the decentralised web. Our challenge is achieving mass adoption while staying true to the principles outlined below."

---

## The 10 Principles

### 1. Liberty
We believe in the sovereignty of individuals. As a research organisation standing for personal liberty, we maximize social, political, and economic freedoms — including being **coercion resistant**.

### 2. Censorship Resistance
We enable the free flow of information. No content is under surveillance. We abide by the cryptoeconomic design principle of censorship resistance. We design **agnostic infrastructures for information**.

### 3. Security
We don't compromise on security when building features. We use state-of-the-art technologies and research new security methods to make strong guarantees.

### 4. Privacy
Privacy is the power to selectively reveal oneself to the world. We protect privacy in both communications and transactions, support pseudo-anonymity, and strive to provide the **right of total anonymity**.

### 5. Transparency
We strive for complete openness and symmetry of information within the organisation, with no border between core contributors and the community. We are frank about shortcomings, especially when making short-term tradeoffs.

### 6. Openness
The software we create is a **public good**. It is available via free and open-source licences, for anyone to share, modify, and benefit from. We believe in permissionless participation.

### 7. Decentralisation
We minimise centralisation across both software and the organisation. We maximise the number of physical computers in the network and the number of individuals who have control over the systems we build.

### 8. Inclusivity
We believe in fair and widespread access, with emphasis on ease of use. This extends to social inclusivity, permissionless participation, interoperability, and educational investment.

### 9. Sustainability
We create software incentivised to **continue to exist and improve without the stewardship of a single entity** or any of the current team members.

### 10. Resourcefulness
We are relentlessly resourceful. As we grow and have access to capital, it is our obligation to fight bureaucracy and inefficiencies — solving problems effectively at lower economic costs (capital, time, resources).

## Relevance

These principles explain Vac's architectural choices: why they build unopinionated protocols, why everything is open-source, why they invest in formal verification and ZK proofs, and why they contribute back to upstream communities (libp2p, Nim ecosystem). The principles are not just marketing — they directly shape what Vac builds and how.
