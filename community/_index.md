# Community Research

Research on the Logos community, movement building, and governance.

## Overview

Logos positions itself as both a **technology stack** and a **social movement** for "revitalising civil society" through decentralised tech.

## Key Components

- **Logos Circles** — local meetup chapters worldwide
- **Logos Operators** — Bitcoin ordinals collection (founding members)
- **Parallel Society Congress** — annual conference
- **Discord** — discord.gg/logosnetwork
- **Forum** — forum.logos.co
