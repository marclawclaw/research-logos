# Logos Overview

> Last updated: 2026-03-14

## What is Logos?

Logos is a **social movement and decentralized technology stack** built to revitalize civil society. It describes itself as "a movement of technologists, creatives, strategists, and activists" dedicated to constructing "a sovereign, people-owned technology stack" that safeguards digital freedoms.

**Mission:** "Restore agency, trust, and civic power by giving people tools to explore and innovate using decentralised technology."

Logos targets "builders, explorers, and dreamers who refuse to accept the world as it is" — specifically those "fed up with corruption, surveillance, and stagnation often associated with incumbent institutions."

**Source:** [logos.co](https://logos.co)

---

## The Technology Stack

Logos comprises three modular, protocol layers — all **private-by-default**:

| Layer | Former Name | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| **Blockchain** | Nomos | Trustless agreements, consensus, privacy-preserving computation |
| **Messaging** | Waku | Peer-to-peer censorship-resistant communication |
| **Storage** | Codex | Decentralized, durable, censorship-resistant data storage |

In late 2025, all three were **unified under the Logos brand**:
- Nomos → **Logos Blockchain**
- Waku → **Logos Messaging**
- Codex → **Logos Storage**

The stack provides "a modular, plugin-based runtime for building decentralised applications" where "nodes can dynamically discover and load modules while preserving privacy."

**Source:** [logos.co/tech-stack](https://logos.co/tech-stack), [Daily Hodl coverage](https://dailyhodl.com/2025/11/20/logos-unifies-under-one-identity-to-deliver-a-private-tech-stack-to-revitalise-civil-society/)

## Organizational Structure

### Institute of Free Technology (IFT)

Logos operates under the **Institute of Free Technology (IFT)**, a mission-driven tech startup studio.

- **Founded:** 2023 (emerging from Status, founded 2017)
- **Co-founders:** Jarrad Hope, Carl Bennetts
- **Team size:** 220+ contributors, fully distributed
- **Core principles:** Liberty, censorship resistance, security, privacy, inclusivity

**IFT Leadership:**

**Logos Programme Lead:** Dr. Corey Petty

**Source:** [free.technology](https://free.technology), [free.technology/logos](https://free.technology/logos)

### IFT Portfolio

IFT incubates multiple projects beyond Logos:

1. **Status** — Blockchain-based app (messaging, wallet, DApp browser, community tools)
2. **Status Network** — Ethereum Layer 2 with public goods funding
3. **Keycard** — Hardware security solution for crypto assets
4. **Nimbus** — Lightweight Ethereum consensus client for solo stakers
5. **Logos** — The decentralized tech stack (Blockchain, Messaging, Storage)

### Relationship to Status

IFT emerged from Status. Status is both a sibling project and a major consumer of Logos technologies — particularly Waku/Messaging for its chat functionality. The nwaku implementation is being integrated into Status Desktop, replacing the older go-waku.

**Source:** [free.technology](https://free.technology)

A newer prototype — self-organizing local groups addressing community issues through grassroots engagement, launched April 2025.

## Analysis

### Strengths
- **Coherent vision:** The unification under one brand (late 2025) signals maturity and strategic clarity
- **Full stack:** Few projects attempt to provide blockchain + messaging + storage as an integrated, privacy-first stack
- **Strong research backing:** Vac provides dedicated R&D with 9 specialized service units
- **Active community:** 28+ Circles globally, regular events, contributor growth

### Concerns
- **Pre-mainnet:** The blockchain layer won't launch mainnet until early 2027 — the stack is not yet production-ready
- **Rebranding confusion:** The recent unification (Nomos→Logos Blockchain, Waku→Logos Messaging, Codex→Logos Storage) may create confusion in documentation and community references. Old URLs (waku.org, codex.storage, nomos.tech, vac.dev) now redirect to logos.co
- **Ambitious scope:** Building all three layers simultaneously is extremely ambitious; execution risk is high
- **Funding opacity:** No public information about funding model, treasury, or token plans (beyond Codex tokenomics litepaper)

### Key Observation
> **Notable:** The 2025 rebranding and URL consolidation is very recent. Many community resources, blog posts, and documentation still reference the old project names. This is a transitional period — expect inconsistency in sources.
