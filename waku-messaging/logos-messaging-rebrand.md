# Waku → Logos Messaging Rebrand (November 2025)

## Summary

In November 2025, **Waku was officially rebranded as Logos Messaging** as part of a coordinated consolidation of the Logos technology stack under a single unified identity. Formerly fragmented as Codex, Nomos, and Waku, all three protocols are now unified under the **Logos** brand.

## Key Facts

- **Announcement:** November 2025, at Devconnect Buenos Aires + Funding the Commons BA 2025
- **Announced by:** Jarrad Hope (Logos co-founder)
- **New name:** Logos Messaging (formerly Waku)
- **Other renames:** Codex → Logos Storage, Nomos → Logos Blockchain (implied)
- **Docs:** Still at https://docs.waku.org during transition; eventually migrating
- **Community:** Discord merge in progress; still separate Waku Discord during transition
- **Codebase:** `nwaku` repository redirects to `logos-messaging/logos-delivery`

## What Changed

- Brand identity and website consolidation
- Mission statement now explicitly tied to "digital sovereignty" and "rebuilding civil society"
- Technical stack, protocols, and open-source approach **unchanged**
- Blog moved from waku.org → blog.waku.org (same content, new framing)

## What Stayed the Same

- All Waku protocols (Relay, Filter, Store, LightPush, RLN) remain identical
- SDKs (js-waku, nwaku / logos-delivery) continue under same repos
- Still open-source and available to any application
- docs.waku.org remains the developer reference during transition

## Strategic Significance

The rebrand reflects Logos treating its tech stack as a coherent **infrastructure suite** rather than independent sibling projects. For Franck's work, this means:

- Logos is positioning itself more explicitly as a movement (not just tools)
- Waku/Logos Messaging is the communication layer of that infrastructure
- Easier to pitch the full stack together: messaging + storage + consensus
